# Project Summary

## Project
Video Game Sales & Market Performance Analytics

## Business question
How can historical video game sales and ratings data be transformed into meaningful insights to identify market leaders, understand regional differences, and explore factors associated with commercial success?

## Workflow
1. Imported the Kaggle Video Game Sales with Ratings dataset into Power BI.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data in Power Query.
3. Created a release identifier and reshaped regional sales into a Region / Regional_Sales structure.
4. Prepared fact and dimension tables for the report model.
5. Created DAX measures, including Total Global Sales.
6. Built two interactive dashboards: Executive Overview and Sales & Regional Intelligence.
7. Interpreted findings while documenting limitations of historical unit-sales data.

## Report pages
- Executive Overview Dashboard
- Sales & Regional Intelligence

## Important interpretation note
The critic-score scatter chart explores association only. It does not prove that critic scores cause sales performance. Sales are unit sales, not revenue or profit.
